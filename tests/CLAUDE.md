# tests

GUT suite, run headless (command in root `CLAUDE.md`). Tests that change `Tuning.field` / `Tuning.ship` restore them; saves go to `user://`, never `res://presets`.

- Smoke: instancing `res://scenes/Run.tscn` yields a scripted `Ship` and `Body` children with `index` 0, 1, 2; `application/run/main_scene` is `res://scenes/Run.tscn`.
- Autoloads and stub scenes/UI/shaders are checked to exist and load; `HUD` is checked as an empty Control root.
- `test_gravity_field.gd`: field math (orbit formula with relative angular speed, tick clock, D35 single-planet default, −∇Φ ≈ accel by finite differences, moon fade bounds and continuity, determinism, `dominant`, `body_vel`, `circular_speed`), rotating-frame math (`frame_omega`, `frame_accel`, `potential_eff` maximum at the carrier orbit, Jacobi integral conserved through `ShipFlight.step`) and the D35 calibration numbers on its own `FieldParams`.
- `test_body.gd`: `Body` places itself at `body_pos(index, time())` on a physics tick; `visual_scale` (constant screen size, smaller moon marker).
- `test_d35_integration.gd`: float32 precision proxy at the D35 scale: rest at the carrier holds for minutes, band drift at the bottom holds its radius.
- `test_ship_flight.gd`: `ShipFlight.step` on its own fields: band-drift orbit keeps its radius, rest at the carrier stays put, thrust along the nose, determinism and non-mutation of inputs, spin damping and heading convergence, `rot` stays wrapped, thrust ramp (weak first tick at `ramp_min_fraction`, full over `ramp_time`, reset on release), heat (growth, cooldown, clamp, thrust falloff, brakes never heat), boost and fuel ratios on applied delta-v, `reference_body` and `sync_reference` selection, soft brake geometry and clamp, drift sync with carrier and body references, branch priority soft brake > drift sync > boost > thrust, and the drift-sync fallback converging to the band drift from both velocity directions above and below the carrier orbit on a preset field.
- `test_ship.gd`: respawn at rest at the carrier with the nose away from the planet (also when a moon dominates there, and on `preset_applied`; a direct field change does not respawn), `step` gets `GravityField.time()` and the fixed `DT` whatever the callback argument, `ship.input` is the input passed to `step`, action mapping in `_read_input`, stick heading is screen-relative.
- `test_ship_prediction.gd`: `ShipPrediction.run` from tick T equals real flight point for point (thrust, soft brake, no input), inputs not mutated; the `Prediction` line is always shown (full `predict_short_steps` points with no input), computed with held turn zeroed, recomputed every `predict_every` ticks (0 is safe) and on `preset_applied`, screen-constant width.
- `test_ship_params.gd`, `test_ship_types.gd`, `test_input_actions.gd`: `Tuning.ship` defaults (removed params stay absent), `copy()` completeness of `ShipState` / `ShipInput`, presence and bindings of the input actions.
- `test_flight_camera.gd`: `rotation_for` in all quadrants, zoom clamp and stop schedule.
- `test_well_view.gd`: `orbit_radii`, highlight threshold, zoom conversions, screen-constant strokes (world = px * s), `visible_span` arc culling for any radius, carrier marker, orbit styles.
- `test_run_scene.gd`: exactly one camera at Run level (`ignore_rotation` false) and its local-vertical behavior, zoom mapping, ship icon scaling without touching physics nodes, three `Body` nodes that hide when the field has fewer bodies, `ReferenceMarker` on the sync reference and hidden without one, screen-constant ring, `FlightVector` statics and presence, hidden `TuningPanel` on a `CanvasLayer`.
- `test_tuning_presets.gd`: save / load round trip, `Tuning.apply` (any body count, deep copy, signal), preset layout (planet plus two moons, explicit D35 constants, moon speeds from the circular-orbit formula, planet mass from the equal-drop recipe), ship sub-resources equal to the code defaults, equal Φ_eff drop across presets, D35 defaults of a fresh `Tuning`.
- `test_tuning_panel.gd`: slider edits reach `GravityField` / `Tuning`, exact slider set (and the non-slider pin), ranges, defaults inside ranges, preset list and Load / Save without write-back, no control takes focus (Space never presses a panel button), only `debug_tuning_panel` toggles visibility, body rows hidden on a one-body field.
