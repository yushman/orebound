# tests

GUT suite, run headless (command in root `CLAUDE.md`). Tests that change `Tuning.field` / `Tuning.ship` / `Tuning.planet` restore them; saves go to `user://`, never `res://presets`. Flight tests on small toy fields (inside the default `planet_surface_radius`) zero `atmosphere_drag` / `atmosphere_damage` or shrink the radii, since the default atmosphere and crash rule treat them as inside the planet.

- Smoke: instancing `res://scenes/Run.tscn` yields a scripted `Ship` and `Body` children with `index` 0, 1, 2; `application/run/main_scene` is `res://scenes/Run.tscn`.
- Autoloads and stub scenes/UI/shaders are checked to exist and load (shaders: `MODE_CANVAS_ITEM`, not their code); `HUD` is checked as an empty Control root.
- `test_gravity_field.gd`: field math (orbit formula with relative angular speed, tick clock, D35 single-planet default, −∇Φ ≈ accel by finite differences, moon fade bounds and continuity, determinism, `dominant`, `body_vel`, `circular_speed`), rotating-frame math (`frame_omega`, `frame_accel`, `potential_eff` maximum at the carrier orbit, Jacobi integral conserved through `ShipFlight.step`), `planet_potential_eff` (ignores moons, equals the planet-only field, strictly increasing on [`atmosphere_edge`, `carrier_orbit_radius`]) and the D35 calibration numbers on its own `FieldParams`.
- `test_body.gd`: `Body` places itself at `body_pos(index, time())` on a physics tick; `visual_scale` (constant screen size, smaller moon marker).
- `test_d35_integration.gd`: float32 precision proxy at the D35 scale: rest at the carrier holds for minutes, band drift at the bottom holds its radius.
- `test_ship_flight.gd`: `ShipFlight.step` on its own fields: band-drift orbit keeps its radius, rest at the carrier stays put, thrust along the nose, determinism and non-mutation of inputs, spin damping and heading convergence, `rot` stays wrapped, thrust ramp (weak first tick at `ramp_min_fraction`, full over `ramp_time`, reset on release), heat (growth, cooldown, clamp, thrust falloff, brakes never heat), boost and fuel ratios on applied delta-v, `reference_body` and `sync_reference` selection, `atmosphere_density` (0 at the edge, 1 at the surface, shape exponent), atmospheric drag (exp factor inside, absent above the edge, band-drift spiral down; the file zeroes drag and damage in `before_each`), hull damage only inside the atmosphere and `crashed` on surface contact or hull 0, soft brake geometry and clamp, drift sync with carrier and body references, branch priority soft brake > drift sync > boost > thrust, and the drift-sync fallback converging to the band drift from both velocity directions above and below the carrier orbit on a preset field.
- `test_ship.gd`: respawn at rest at the carrier with the nose away from the planet (also when a moon dominates there, and on `preset_applied`; a direct field change does not respawn), `step` gets `GravityField.time()` and the fixed `DT` whatever the callback argument, `ship.input` is the input passed to `step`, action mapping in `_read_input`, stick heading is screen-relative, a crashed step respawns at the carrier with full hull and `Telemetry.crashes` + 1, a preset change restores hull without touching the counter (tests restore `Telemetry.crashes`).
- `test_ship_prediction.gd`: `ShipPrediction.run` from tick T equals real flight point for point (thrust, soft brake, no input), inputs not mutated; truncation at the first crashed state (crash point last, equal to flight up to it); the `Prediction` line is always shown (full `predict_short_steps` points with no input), computed with held turn zeroed, recomputed every `predict_every` ticks (0 is safe) and on `preset_applied`, screen-constant width.
- `test_ship_params.gd`, `test_ship_types.gd`, `test_input_actions.gd`: `Tuning.ship` defaults incl. `hull_max` (removed params stay absent), `copy()` completeness of `ShipState` / `ShipInput`, presence and bindings of the input actions (incl. zoom keys).
- `test_flight_camera.gd`: `rotation_for` in all quadrants, zoom clamp and stop schedule.
- `test_well_view.gd`: atmosphere stroke has the `GLOW_COLOR` hue, `orbit_radii`, highlight threshold, zoom conversions, screen-constant strokes (world = px * s), `visible_span` arc culling for any radius, carrier marker, orbit styles, `boundary_style` distinct from every `orbit_style`, `special_rings` on generator radii, and a pin that WellView no longer fills tiers.
- `test_gravity_halo_view.gd`: `build_uniforms` packing (moons only, camera-relative, padded to 16, zero-radius moon skipped, field read per call), `ring_phase` moves rings inward and faster near the moon, `halo_alpha` profile reading `Tuning.view` per call, shader parameters pushed by `GravityHaloView` (incl. `Tuning.view` values, per refresh and on `preset_applied`), the halo shader declares them.
- `test_view_params.gd`: `Tuning.view` defaults (`ViewParams`: `halo_alpha`, `ring_count`, `ring_period`, `ring_px`, `ring_alpha`), untouched by `Tuning.apply` / save / load, no view field in `TuningPreset`.
- `test_atmosphere_view.gd`: `AtmosphereView.density` equals `ShipFlight.atmosphere_density`, shader parameters pushed by `AtmosphereView`, the atmosphere shader declares them.
- `test_planet_params.gd`: `Tuning.planet` defaults (tier shares, ceiling offset, moon and ring placeholders, atmosphere drag / damage / density shape); `Tuning.apply` keeps the planet.
- `test_well_generator.gd`: `WellGenerator.tiers()` for p1–p4 and the default field: boundaries strictly ascending from atmosphere edge to carrier orbit, adjacent Φ_eff difference = share × swing (relative tolerance on Φ), inner radii differ between presets, tow = `boundaries[2]`, ceiling = carrier orbit + `ceiling_offset` and follows a changed offset (no cache).
- `test_run_scene.gd`: `AtmosphereView`, `GravityHaloView`, `WellView` first in that order with shader materials, no `IsolineView` child and no `scenes/IsolineView.gd` / `shaders/isolines.gdshader` resources, exactly one camera at Run level (`ignore_rotation` false) and its local-vertical behavior, zoom mapping, ship icon scaling without touching physics nodes, three `Body` nodes that hide when the field has fewer bodies, `ReferenceMarker` on the sync reference and hidden without one, screen-constant ring, `FlightVector` statics and presence, hidden `TuningPanel` on a `CanvasLayer`.
- `test_tuning_presets.gd`: save / load round trip, `Tuning.apply` (any body count, deep copy, signal), preset layout (planet plus two moons, explicit D35 constants, moon speeds from the circular-orbit formula, planet mass from the equal-drop recipe), ship sub-resources equal to the code defaults, equal Φ_eff drop across presets, D35 defaults of a fresh `Tuning`.
- `test_tuning_panel.gd`: slider edits reach `GravityField` / `Tuning`, exact slider set incl. `planet.*`, `view.*` and `ship.hull_max` (and the non-slider pin), ranges, ship / planet / view defaults inside ranges, planet sliders write into `Tuning.planet`, view sliders (`halo_alpha`, `ring_count`, `ring_period`, `ring_px`, `ring_alpha`) write into `Tuning.view`, readout (hull, density, crashes; no error without a Ship), preset list and Load / Save without write-back, no control takes focus (Space never presses a panel button), only `debug_tuning_panel` toggles visibility, body rows hidden on a one-body field.
