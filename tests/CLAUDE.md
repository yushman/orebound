# tests

GUT suite, run headless (command in root `CLAUDE.md`). Tests that change `Tuning.field` / `Tuning.ship` / `Tuning.planet` / `Tuning.picture` restore them (and `Telemetry` when a regenerate writes it); tests that run `ShipFlight` / `ShipPrediction` where asteroids would interfere pin `asteroids_per_band = 0` and every `small_rate` to 0 (saved / restored `Tuning.planet`; small bodies would otherwise spawn and disturb flight and prediction) and set `Tuning.picture` by hand; saves go to `user://`, never `res://presets`. Flight tests on small toy fields (inside the default `planet_surface_radius`) zero `atmosphere_drag` / `atmosphere_damage` or shrink the radii, since the default atmosphere and crash rule treat them as inside the planet.

- Smoke: instancing `res://scenes/Run.tscn` yields a scripted `Ship` and `Body` children with `index` 0..3 (root has `Run.gd`); `application/run/main_scene` is `res://scenes/Run.tscn`.
- Autoloads and stub scenes/UI/shaders are checked to exist and load (shaders: `MODE_CANVAS_ITEM`, not their code); `HUD` is checked as a Control root (scripted, see `ui/CLAUDE.md`).
- `test_gravity_field.gd`: field math (orbit formula with relative angular speed, tick clock, D35 single-planet default, −∇Φ ≈ accel by finite differences, moon fade bounds and continuity, determinism, `dominant`, `body_vel`, `circular_speed`), rotating-frame math (`frame_omega`, `frame_accel`, `potential_eff` maximum at the carrier orbit, Jacobi integral conserved through `ShipFlight.step`), `planet_potential_eff` (ignores moons, equals the planet-only field, strictly increasing on [`atmosphere_edge`, `carrier_orbit_radius`]) and the D35 calibration numbers on its own `FieldParams`.
- `test_body.gd`: `Body` places itself at `body_pos(index, time())` on a physics tick; the planet is a disc of `planet_surface_radius` in `OBOD_ACCENT` with a `Lit` half-disc (no constant-screen-size square, no `visual_scale`), a moon a grey `Palette.MOON` disc of its radius with a shaded dark side and a lit half, coarser than the planet disc (fewer points).
- `test_palette.gd`: the meaning-bound colour constants, `MOON` neutral, `shade` multiplies rgb by 0.35 keeping alpha, `LIGHT_DIR` is a unit vector, `lit_half` has n+1 points on the lit side and follows the light direction, and the `default_clear_color` project setting equals `Palette.BACKGROUND`, `HULL` / `HULL_SHADE` are set, `FUEL` is #8FE0A0.
- `test_ship_view.gd`: `flame_length` (0 without thrust or boost, ramps with `hold_time`, falls above `heat_threshold`, longer when boosting), `glow_alpha` (0 at heat 0, 1 at heat 1, monotone, faint below the threshold and steep after); on the instanced `Ship/Visual`: the view script sits on `Visual`, the flame length follows `ship.input` / `ship.state` in `Palette.DANGER`, the view never writes flight state, glow alpha / colour / size follow heat, hull and wing colours are `HULL` / `HULL_SHADE`, the flame is off while `drift_sync` or `soft_brake` is held, a hidden flame does not reassign the polygon.
- `test_d35_integration.gd`: float32 precision proxy at the D35 scale: rest at the carrier holds for minutes, band drift at the bottom holds its radius.
- `test_ship_flight.gd`: `ShipFlight.step` on its own fields: band-drift orbit keeps its radius, a ship resting on a moon or asteroid keeps its hull while a hard impact still bounces and damages, rest at the carrier stays put, thrust along the nose, determinism and non-mutation of inputs, spin damping and heading convergence, `rot` stays wrapped, thrust ramp (weak first tick at `ramp_min_fraction`, full over `ramp_time`, reset on release), heat (growth, cooldown, clamp, thrust falloff, brakes never heat), boost and fuel ratios on applied delta-v, `reference_body` and `sync_reference` selection, `atmosphere_density` (0 at the edge, 1 at the surface, shape exponent), atmospheric drag (exp factor inside, absent above the edge, band-drift spiral down; the file zeroes drag and damage in `before_each`), hull damage only inside the atmosphere and `crashed` on surface contact or hull 0, soft brake geometry and clamp, drift sync with carrier and body references, branch priority soft brake > drift sync > boost > thrust, the drift-sync fallback converging to the band drift from both velocity directions above and below the carrier orbit on a preset field, moon contact (inside the disc: pushed to the surface, normal velocity reflected, hull damaged; outside unchanged; a zero-radius body never collides), asteroid contact (same, `crashed` never set) and drift sync to an asteroid (relative velocity zeroed).
- `test_ship.gd`: respawn at rest at the carrier with the nose away from the planet (also when a moon dominates there, and on `preset_applied`; a direct field change does not respawn), `step` gets `GravityField.time()` and the fixed `DT` whatever the callback argument, `ship.input` is the input passed to `step`, action mapping in `_read_input`, stick heading is screen-relative, `respawned` is emitted by `respawn()`, a crashed step respawns at the carrier with full hull and `Telemetry.crashes` + 1, a preset change restores hull without touching the counter (tests restore `Telemetry.crashes`).
- `test_ship_prediction.gd`: `ShipPrediction.run` from tick T equals real flight point for point (thrust, soft brake, no input), inputs not mutated; truncation at the first crashed state (crash point last, equal to flight up to it); equality through a moon bounce and through an asteroid bounce, equality also when accelerating into asteroids; 900 steps with asteroids cost < 3 ms over a no-asteroid baseline (median of runs: dense field, high-speed start, accelerating); the `Prediction` line is always shown (full `predict_short_steps` points with no input), computed with held turn zeroed, recomputed every `predict_every` ticks (0 is safe) and on the ship's `respawned` (also after a crash respawn with `predict_every` > 1), screen-constant width.
- `test_ship_params.gd`, `test_ship_types.gd`, `test_input_actions.gd`: `Tuning.ship` defaults incl. `hull_max` (removed params stay absent), `copy()` completeness of `ShipState` / `ShipInput`, presence and bindings of the input actions (incl. zoom keys).
