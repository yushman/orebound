# gen

Pure static generators for the well; no node state, no cache.

- `WellGenerator.tiers() -> Dictionary` is stage 1. It reads `Tuning.field` and `Tuning.planet` on every call and root-finds on `GravityField.planet_potential_eff`. Result: `boundaries: Array[float]` (5 ascending radii: `atmosphere_edge`, then 10 %, 35 %, 75 % of the Φ_eff swing above the edge, then `carrier_orbit_radius`; cumulative `tier_shares` from the bottom), `tow` (= `boundaries[2]`, bottom of the middle tier) and `ceiling` (= `carrier_orbit_radius + ceiling_offset`).
- swing = Φp(`carrier_orbit_radius`) − Φp(`atmosphere_edge`); each inner boundary is found by bisection on [edge, r_c] and stops on a Φ tolerance relative to the swing, not an r tolerance: `frame_omega` uses `eps` while Φ does not, so g_eff(r_c) is only ≈ 0 and the profile is flat near r_c.
- `WellGenerator.tier_of(r) -> int` is the tier index (0 bottom .. 3 top) from `tiers()["boundaries"]`, clamped: 0 below the atmosphere edge, 3 above the carrier orbit.
- `WellGenerator.picture(seed, flight) -> Dictionary` (`{seed, flight, moons: Array[BodyParams], ring: {radius, width, phase}}`; `ring = {}` when no gap fits) builds the moons and the ring. `picture` computes `tiers()` once and passes the boundaries to `_build(seed, flight, bounds = [])` (static, test-visible; empty `bounds` means it computes them itself) for every retry and the fallback. `_build` seeds a `RandomNumberGenerator` with `hash([seed, flight])`, so a picture is deterministic and the same call gives the same result. Moon count is `randi_range(moon_count_min, moon_count_max)` (at least 1): one large moon (`radius` in `moon_large_radius_min/max`, orbit between `boundaries[3]` and `boundaries[4]`) and the rest small (`moon_small_radius_min/max`, orbit between `boundaries[1]` and `boundaries[3]`). Each moon has `parent` 0, p = 2, random `phase`, `mass = moon_mass_k · radius^moon_mass_exp`, `influence_radius = moon_influence_k · radius` and `angular_speed` of a circular orbit on the planet pull at its orbit radius (`accel_of(0, ...)`).
- Ring: after the moons `_ring` takes the tier `boundaries[ring_tier]..boundaries[ring_tier+1]` (`ring_tier` indexes `boundaries` bottom-up, unlike the top-down `tier_shares`), cuts out every moon annulus `orbit ± (influence_radius + moon_margin)`, keeps the gaps at least `ring_width` wide, picks one, a random radial offset inside it and a random `phase`. The ring is its own band: one Ω at its centre radius.
- `WellGenerator.relative_omega(r)` = `sqrt(g_planet(r)/r) − GravityField.frame_omega()` (planet-only g, as in `_moon`), read per call; ~0 at the carrier orbit. Rings, bands and asteroids use it.
- `WellGenerator.check(moons, ring = null) -> bool` accepts a layout when every moon clears the atmosphere (`orbit_radius − radius > atmosphere_edge`), the carrier orbit (`|r_c − orbit_radius| > influence_radius + moon_margin`) and every other moon (orbit gap > sum of influence radii + `moon_margin`). With a `ring` argument it also rejects an empty ring and a ring band crossing a moon annulus (`|ring.radius − orbit_radius| < influence_radius + moon_margin + width/2`); `check(moons)` alone keeps the moon-only result. `picture` tries `seed`, `seed + 1`, ... up to `max_tries` and returns the first layout passing `check(moons, ring)`; if none passes it builds `fallback_seed` (it is not checked again; a test pins that `fallback_seed` 2 passes on the default field and p1..p4 with the ring; seed 1 fails the ring-aware check). The returned `seed` is the seed actually used, so it may differ from the requested one.
- `AsteroidField` (`gen/AsteroidField.gd`, static, `RefCounted`) is the lazy asteroid generator; it reads `Tuning.planet`, `Tuning.field` and `Tuning.picture` on every call, no cache. Bands of `band_width` run from `atmosphere_edge` to the ceiling: `band_count()`, `band_of(r)` (clamped to `band_count() − 1`), `band_centre(k)` and `band_omega(k) = WellGenerator.relative_omega(band_centre(k))` (the last band may extend past the ceiling). Every object in a band rotates rigidly with the band Ω: one band per tier would shear visibly (relative Ω varies across a tier), and an Ω per object would break chunk content. `band_blocked(k)` is true when the band intersects a moon annulus (`orbit ± (influence_radius + moon_margin)`) or the ring band; such bands have no fields (time-independent, since bands and moons rotate at different Ω). The band is widened by `field_radius + ast_radius_max` on each side, so a field placed near a band edge cannot reach a moon zone or the ring.
- Chunk = fixed arc in its band: `chunk_count(k) = round(2π·r_k/chunk_arc)` (at least 1), `Δθ = 2π/n`. `chunk(k, i, bands = -1, bounds = [])` is a pure function of `Tuning.picture` `seed` / `flight`, `k`, `i` (RNG `hash([seed, flight, k, i])`; `bands` only skips a recount, `bounds` are the tier boundaries, computed once by `near()` and passed in, empty means `chunk` fetches them from `WellGenerator.tiers()`): 0 or 1 field of 3..6 attempted asteroids (`FIELD_MIN/MAX`) inside `field_radius`, dart-throwing Poisson-disk placement (`TRIES` per asteroid), each asteroid a convex hull of random points (distance 0.85..1.0 of the radius from the centre, so the hull stays within `radius` and the contact circle) with radius in `ast_radius_min..ast_radius_max`. Entries are `{r, theta, radius, points, type, amount}` at t = 0. `type` is a `Tuning.resources` key by tier (an inline clamped tier walk over `bounds`, same result as `WellGenerator.tier_of(r)`: 0 `ore_unique`, 1 `ore_rare`, 2 `ore_middle`, 3 `ice` with probability `top_ice_share` else `ore_base`), decided by a hash draw `(hash([seed, flight, k, i, j, "comp"]) & 0xFFFFFF) / 2^24 < top_ice_share`, not by the chunk RNG, so hull geometry does not shift; `amount` = hull area (shoelace) × `amount_per_area` (cells). The chance of a field is `band_expected(k)` over the expected asteroids per chunk: `asteroids_per_band` for bands at least `drop_start` bands (`drop_start` is measured in bands from the carrier orbit) below the carrier, then each band closer to the carrier ×`drop_ratio`, bands above the carrier ×`above_factor` (so above the carrier the density is `asteroids_per_band · drop_ratio^drop_start · above_factor`). A field is placed at a random point in its chunk and may reach into neighbours.
- Motion is a formula of t: `state(k, ast, t)` / `at(ast, t)` give `pos = r·(cos, sin)(theta + Ω·t)` and `vel = Ω × pos`; `chunk_index(k, angle, t) = floor((angle − Ω·t)/Δθ) mod n`, so chunk membership never changes with t. `max_speed()` is the largest band speed.
- `near(pos, t, R) -> Array` returns exactly the asteroids within R of `pos` as `{pos, vel, radius, points, type, amount, band, chunk, idx, r, theta, omega}` (`r`, `theta` at t = 0 and the band `omega` let a caller move the entry to any t without regeneration). It visits only the bands and chunks the circle can reach (neighbour chunks and bands included). Cost: generating chunks is the expensive part (tech-debt #6), so callers with many steps take one `near` snapshot. `AsteroidField.max_speed()` is the largest band speed `|band_omega|·(band_centre + band_width/2 + field_radius)`; callers use it to bound how far an asteroid moves in a horizon.
- `SmallObjectSchedule` (`gen/SmallObjectSchedule.gd`, static, `RefCounted`) is the pure spawn schedule of small bodies, M4; it reads `Tuning.planet` per call, no cache. `schedule(seed, flight, tier, window) -> Array` returns the events of one window `[window · small_window, (window + 1) · small_window)` as `{time, angle, e, nu, radius, points, type, amount, id}`; RNG `hash([seed, flight, "small", tier, window])`, a separate stream, so `AsteroidField` chunks are unchanged. Gaps are exponential (Poisson), mean count = `small_rate[tier]` (events/min, `tier` 0 bottom .. 3 top like `tier_of`) × window length; rate 0 gives no events. `e` is in [`small_e_min`, `small_e_max`], `radius` in [`small_radius_min`, `small_radius_max`], `points` a convex hull from the private `AsteroidField._hull`, `type` by tier with the asteroid composition rule, `amount` = area × `amount_per_area`, `id` a hash of seed, flight, tier, window and event index. `SmallObjects.update` takes the events whose time is in (t − dt, t].
