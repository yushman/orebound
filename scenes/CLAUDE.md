# scenes

Game scenes.

- `Run.tscn` (Node2D, main scene, `Run.gd`) holds, in this draw order, `AtmosphereView`, `GravityHaloView`, `WellView`, `RingView`, `AsteroidView`, 4 `Body.tscn` instances (`Planet` index 0, `Moon1`..`Moon3` index 1..3; a moon node hides when the generated picture has fewer moons; discs draw over glow, halo, strokes, ring and asteroids and under the ship and overlays), an instance of `Ship.tscn`, the `Prediction` `Line2D`, `ReferenceMarker`, `FlightVector`, the run-level `FlightCamera` and a `CanvasLayer` with a `ui/TuningPanel.tscn` instance (hidden on start, screen-fixed) and a `ui/HUD.tscn` instance (well scale, ignores mouse and focus).
- `Run.gd` (Node2D root of `Run.tscn`): `_ready` calls `Tuning.regenerate(Telemetry.seed, Telemetry.flight)`, so a fresh start runs a generated planet-plus-moons picture (seed 1, flight 1) instead of the planet-only code default. It owns nothing else.
- `Ship.tscn` is a `CharacterBody2D` with `Ship.gd`, a `Visual` `Node2D` (script `ShipView.gd`; hull of three `Polygon2D`: `Hull`, `WingLeft`, `WingRight`, nose toward local -Y) and a `CollisionShape2D` with a `CircleShape2D` (unchanged). It has no camera: there is one camera, `FlightCamera`.
- `Palette.gd` (`class_name Palette`, `RefCounted`) is the one colour table (meaning-bound, style frame W7): `BACKGROUND` #0B0F1A (equals the `default_clear_color` project setting, pinned by a test), `ICE` #7FC8F8 (ring, top asteroid tier), `ORE` #F2A93B (cargo, asteroids), `ORE_BRIGHT` #FFE27A (unique ore), `DANGER` #FF5A36 (atmosphere glow and edge, ceiling, flame, nozzle heat), `HALO` #B8D8C0 (gravity halo, orbits, prediction, velocity vector, reference marker, UI lines), `SAFE` #FFD45C (carrier orbit and marker, tow boundary, HUD ship marker), `OBOD_ACCENT` #C9A27A (planet disc), `MOON` neutral grey, `HULL` (ship hull) and `HULL_SHADE` (wings). Static `shade(c)` multiplies rgb by `SHADE_FACTOR` 0.35 (alpha kept); `LIGHT_DIR` is the unit Vector2 to the star in the carrier's rotating frame (fixed there, so on screen the terminator turns with the camera; tech-debt #35); `lit_half(radius, n, light_dir) -> PackedVector2Array` gives n+1 points of the lit half-circle (terminator through the centre perpendicular to `light_dir`). Views read their hue from `Palette` and keep their own alpha and stroke constants; shader uniform defaults match it.
- Flight physics is the pure `ShipFlight.step(state, input, t, dt) -> ShipState` (`class_name`, static, no scene dependency, so trajectory prediction can reuse it). It returns a new state, never mutates its arguments, and is deterministic. `ShipParams` are read from `Tuning.ship` on each call, no cache. Any new flight feature goes into the state and `step`, not into the `Ship` node.
- Step order: rotation, control branch, heat, atmospheric drag, translation, moon contact, asteroid contact, hull and crash. The control branch is an elif chain, soft brake > drift sync > boost / thrust; held actions do not add up. Rotation is manual `turn` or, with `has_heading`, a critically damped PD toward `target_heading`; spin damping is always on; `rot` stays wrapped to [-PI, PI]. Thrust acts along the nose `Vector2.UP.rotated(rot)`. Translation is semi-implicit Euler in the rotating frame: gravity, `frame_accel`, thrust and brake terms, then position. A ship at rest at the carrier stays there. Accepted cost: explicit Coriolis in semi-implicit Euler drifts band-drift orbits at large Ω_f; D35-scale Ω_f is not affected.
- Thrust, ramp and heat (D36): holding the engine (thrust or boost) grows `hold_time`; the thrust scale is `lerp(ramp_min_fraction, 1, hold_time / ramp_time)`, so a short tap is a weak but non-zero impulse. Every other branch resets `hold_time`. `heat` in [0, 1] grows on the engine (faster on boost) and cools on every non-engine branch, so brakes never heat; above `heat_threshold` thrust falls linearly to `min_thrust_fraction` at full heat (boost inherits). `boost` alone gives `thrust_accel·boost_accel_mult`. Fuel is per actually applied delta-v with a separate counter per mode (`fuel_engine`, `fuel_boost`, `fuel_soft_brake`, `fuel_sync`); fuel only accumulates, there is no tank limit.
- Atmosphere: static `ShipFlight.atmosphere_density(pos)` = `clamp((edge − |pos|)/(edge − surface), 0, 1) ^ density_shape`, radii from `Tuning.field`, shape from `Tuning.planet`, read per call; 0 at and above the edge, 1 at the surface.
- Atmospheric drag: in `step`, right before the velocity update, `vel *= exp(−atmosphere_drag·ρ·dt)` with ρ at the current position, for every control branch. The atmosphere co-rotates with the carrier frame, so rotating-frame `vel` is the velocity relative to the air. Unforced flight low in the atmosphere spirals down (default drag 0.5: about −3.7 u in 900 steps from r = 101250 on the D35 field). The default drag must give a measurable descent: float32 positions round radial motion slower than ~0.25 u/s per tick to zero (`docs/production/tech-debt.md` #11), so a weak drag stalls the spiral. Accepted cost: drift sync and its band-drift fallback fight the drag inside the atmosphere at fuel cost.
- Hull and crash: after the position update `hull = max(hull − atmosphere_damage·ρ·dt, 0)` with ρ at the new position (no damage above the edge) and `crashed = |pos| <= planet_surface_radius or hull <= 0`. `step` only sets the flag and the caller respawns, so the crash point stays visible to prediction and `step` owns no counter or node.
- Moon contact (`_moon_contact`): after the position update, for every body with `radius` > 0 (moons) at `GravityField.body_pos(i, t)`: if the ship is inside the disc it is put on the surface along the outward normal, and if it moves into the surface (velocity relative to `body_vel` has a negative normal part v_n) the normal part is reflected with restitution `moon_bounce` and `hull −= moon_impact_damage · |v_n|` (floored at 0). Both contacts share `_contact(s, n, v_n, bounce, damage)`: when `-v_n < Tuning.planet.rest_speed` (default 0.3) the ship is resting, only the normal part of the relative velocity is cancelled (no bounce, no damage), so lying on a surface does not drain hull; a harder impact bounces and damages as above. A moon never sets `crashed` by itself (only the planet surface or hull 0 does). Inside `step`, so prediction == flight and the prediction line bounces off moons; `Tuning.planet` is read per call. Accepted cost: push-out against a moving disc may jitter; a prediction == flight test guards determinism.
- Asteroid contact (`_asteroid_contact`): right after `_moon_contact`, for every asteroid within `max(ast_radius_min, ast_radius_max)` of the ship (`AsteroidField.near`, or the snapshot below) the asteroid is a circle of its `radius` at its position at t: inside it the ship is put on the surface along the outward normal, and a negative normal part v_n of the velocity relative to the asteroid `vel` is reflected with `asteroid_bounce`, `hull −= asteroid_impact_damage · |v_n|` (floored at 0). It never sets `crashed`. The `Polygon2D` of `AsteroidView` is visual only; no physics-engine colliders (they would diverge prediction from flight). Resting on an asteroid is handled by the shared `_contact` helper like moons (`rest_speed`: normal cancelled, no bounce, no damage).
- Asteroid snapshot: `ShipFlight.step(state, input, t, dt, snapshot = null)`: without a snapshot (live flight) the asteroid queries are `AsteroidField.near(pos, t, R)` once per query; with one, its entries (`near` output carrying `r`, `theta`, `omega`) are moved to t by formula (`AsteroidField.at`) and filtered by the same radius, with identical results. `sync_reference(pos, t, snapshot = null)` takes it too.
- Soft brake (`ship_brake`) lives in `step`, so prediction stays equal to flight. Its acceleration is half of `brake_accel` against velocity plus half backwards from the nose; near rest only the nose half is applied. Thrust and boost are ignored while it is held, rotation still works, no heat.
- Drift sync (`ship_drift_sync`): the reference is the pure `ShipFlight.sync_reference(pos, t, snapshot = null) -> {valid, index, is_carrier, asteroid}`; candidates are all bodies, the carrier point and asteroids within `sync_radius` (`AsteroidField.near`), the nearest by distance to the centre strictly within `sync_radius` wins (a body on a tie); `asteroid` is `{pos, vel}` when an asteroid won and `{}` otherwise (`index` -1, `is_carrier` false), recomputed each step and never stored. With a reference the ship is pulled to the reference velocity (zero for the carrier, `body_vel` for a body, the asteroid `vel` for an asteroid) at no more than `sync_accel`. Thrust and boost are ignored, no heat, rotation works, fuel is more expensive than the engine.
- Drift-sync fallback (no reference within `sync_radius`, `_band_drift_accel`): converge to the band drift of the current depth around `reference_body` (= `GravityField.dominant`). The math is done in the rotating frame: the orbit direction is taken from the inertial relative velocity (so a retrograde ship stays retrograde; near-radial velocity falls back to counter-clockwise) and the target is the circular velocity around the body expressed back in the rotating frame. It is clamped by `sync_accel`, independent of the nose, and billed like sync. Accepted costs: near a moon the planet pull also acts, so the target is not exactly circular; a reference switch jumps the target, smoothed only by the clamp.
- `ReferenceMarker` marks the sync reference: a `Palette.HALO` ring at the reference body, carrier point or asteroid (`ref.asteroid.pos`), hidden when there is no reference. The ring has a constant screen size (world radius = `RING_PX * s`), brighter while `ship.input.drift_sync`. View-only: reads the ship, never writes flight state.
- `FlightCamera.gd` (`class_name`, Camera2D in `Run.tscn`) is the local-vertical camera (D35). Pure statics: `rotation_for(pos)` makes screen-down point at the planet center from any position; semantic zoom s ("s times more world per screen", 1x = 1280x800 units) is clamped to [1, 40], continuous up to 22 and then stops 28 / 34 / 40; `zoom_vector(s)` inverts it for `Camera2D.zoom`. The node follows the ship, rotates by `rotation_for` (`ignore_rotation = false` in `_ready`; Godot 4 defaults to true, which silently drops the rotation), eases s in s-space, and reads `camera_zoom_in` / `camera_zoom_out`. It scales only the ship's `Visual` node (the whole look: hull, flame, glow) by s (constant screen size); `ship.scale` and the collision shape are never touched. No autozoom. View-only.
- `FlightVector.gd` is the velocity arrow from `ship.state.pos` along the current velocity only (no acceleration term), drawn in `Palette.HALO`, its shaft is |v| long in WORLD units (honest 1:1), head and stroke have constant screen size, hidden below 1 u/s. Always shown; the prediction line is separate. View-only.
- `WellView.gd` draws the well as honest arcs: atmosphere edge and `r = const` orbits (the carrier orbit `SAFE`, other orbits `HALO`; bodies with `parent == 0`); the planet surface is not stroked, the planet disc edge (`Body`) is the surface, read from `Tuning.field` on every draw. Stroke widths are constant screen pixels (world = px * s). The carrier orbit keeps its own stronger style at any zoom; other orbits are highlighted when zoomed far out (s >= 22). Special boundaries (`atmosphere`, `tow`, `ceiling`) are solid circles with their own `boundary_style(kind)` (tow `Palette.SAFE`, ceiling and atmosphere `Palette.DANGER`), distinct from every `orbit_style`. `special_rings()` (static) lists the atmosphere edge, tow and ceiling at generator radii, drawn with `boundary_style`. The carrier point is marked at `(carrier_orbit_radius, 0)`. Tiers are not tinted (D37). Arcs are drawn for the visible part only, with a segment count that bounds the chord sagitta. Redraws every frame and on `preset_applied`. View-only.
- `GravityHaloView.gd` (`class_name`, Node2D, world space, before `WellView`) sits at the camera and draws an axis-aligned square of side `2 · WellView.visible_half_extent(cam_zoom) · VIEW_MARGIN` centred on it with a ShaderMaterial (created by the node) on `shaders/gravity_halo.gdshader`: around each moon (`influence_radius` > 0, never the planet) a `Palette.HALO` halo and contracting rings inside `outer = influence_radius · (1 + fade_width)`. With `u = |local − rel| / outer` (u < 1 only): halo alpha `halo_alpha · (1 − u)²`, rings at `fract(ring_count · u^1.5 + ring_time_phase)` with constant `ring_px` screen width via `fwidth` (`x` and `fwidth(x)` are computed before the `u >= 1` check, which only skips the alpha accumulation) and alpha `ring_alpha · (1 − u)`; moons combine by max. Rings move inward, faster near the moon (free-fall time from rest ∝ r^1.5: being pulled in). Statics: `build_uniforms(cam, t)` (`moon_count`, camera-relative `rel` and `outer`, arrays padded to 16; rel is a camera-relative Vector2, float32 with sub-pixel error, not double), `ring_phase(u) = u^1.5`, `advance_phase(phase, delta, period) = fposmod(phase + delta / period, 1.0)` and `halo_alpha(u)` (CPU mirrors of the shader profile, 0 for u ≥ 1). `halo_alpha`, `ring_count`, `ring_period`, `ring_px`, `ring_alpha` come from `Tuning.view` (`ViewParams`), read per refresh, no cache; the colour is `Palette.HALO` (pushed as `halo_color`). The shader does not use `TIME`: `_process` accumulates the phase with `advance_phase` and pushes it as the `ring_time_phase` uniform, so rings do not jump at the time rollover or on a `ring_period` change. Refreshed in `_process` and on `preset_applied`; no error without a camera. View-only.
- The gravity field is shown only by moon halos (D37): Φ_eff isolines are not drawn (float32 noise near the carrier, unreadable when zoomed out), and the planet has no field layer (rings around it would fill the whole well); atmosphere glow and special boundary circles show the planet.
- `RingView.gd` (`class_name`, Node2D) holds a `MultiMeshInstance2D` (`Particles`, 40 u quads, `PARTICLE_COLOR` = `Palette.ICE` at alpha 0.6): `particle_count(density)` = `ring_density` (clamped 0..1) of `MAX_PARTICLES` 4000; radii inside `ring.radius ± width/2`, random angles from RNG `hash([seed, flight, "ring"])` (`particle_positions(ring, n)`, public `positions`). Built once in `_ready` and on each `preset_applied` from `Tuning.picture.ring`, not per frame; an empty ring hides the node and leaves zero instances. `_process` only sets the node `rotation = rotation_at(ring, t) = ring.phase + relative_omega(ring.radius)·t`, so the ring rotates rigidly. Visual only: no collisions, not a sync reference.
- `AsteroidView.gd` (`class_name`, Node2D) is a pool of `Polygon2D` matching `AsteroidField.near(ship.state.pos, t, chunk_gen_radius)` (the Ship is found at `../Ship`; nothing is shown without one). Points come from the asteroid hull, the colour is a placeholder by tier from the palette (`color_for(r)` from `WellGenerator.tiers()["boundaries"]`, `TIER_COLORS` per the well-generator composition: top `ICE`, middle `MOON` stone, lower `ORE`, bottom `ORE_BRIGHT`). The pool is reassigned only when the key list `[band, chunk, idx, radius, points]` changes (also reset on `preset_applied`, so a regenerated field with equal indices is rebuilt) (surplus polygons are hidden, not freed); positions follow t every frame and each polygon rotates by `omega·t` (the band angular speed, as the field turns). Far chunks do not exist, even on screen. View-only.
- `AtmosphereView.gd` (Node2D, first in `Run.tscn`) draws the same camera-centred square with `shaders/atmosphere.gdshader`: `GLOW_COLOR` (the `Palette.DANGER` rgb, shader default aligned) with alpha `ATMO_ALPHA · ρ`, no shimmer. Statics: `build_uniforms(cam)` (`cam`, `surface`, `edge`, `shape` from `Tuning.field` / `Tuning.planet`, per call) and `density(r, u)` equal to `ShipFlight.atmosphere_density`. Refreshed in `_process` and on `preset_applied`. View-only.
- `ShipState` (pose, rotation, fuel counters, `hold_time`, `heat`, `hull` (default `Tuning.ship.hull_max`), `crashed`) and `ShipInput` (`turn`, `has_heading`, `target_heading`, `thrust`, `boost`, `soft_brake`, `drift_sync`) are `RefCounted` with typed fields and a field-by-field `copy()`; prediction == flight depends on `copy()` being complete. Ramp and heat state live in `ShipState`, not on the node. `ShipInput.screen_heading(stick, pos)` converts a gamepad stick to a world heading relative to the camera (adds `FlightCamera.rotation_for(pos)`), so stick-up is screen-up.
- `Ship.gd` holds the authoritative `state`. `signal respawned` is emitted at the end of `respawn()`. `_ready` calls `respawn()`, also connected to `Tuning.preset_applied` (a preset load respawns at the carrier of the new field; generation-affecting slider edits go through `Tuning.regenerate` and respawn too, other slider edits do not). `respawn()` puts a fresh `ShipState` with `hull = Tuning.ship.hull_max` at rest at the carrier point `(carrier_orbit_radius, 0)` with the nose away from the planet (`rot = FlightCamera.rotation_for(pos)`). `_physics_process` builds a `ShipInput` from the actions, keeps it in `input`, steps with the fixed `GravityField.DT` and `GravityField.time()` (read after the tick increment, like `Body`), then, if `state.crashed`, does `Telemetry.crashes += 1` and `respawn()` (no move that tick), else `move_and_collide(state.pos - position)` and `rotation = state.rot`. Accepted cost: with no colliders `state.pos` is the node position; once something collides they diverge and need syncing.
- `ShipPrediction.run(state, input, tick, steps) -> PackedVector2Array` (`class_name`, static, pure) repeats `ShipFlight.step` with `t = GravityField.time_at(tick + i + 1)`, returning the position after each step; it stops at the first `crashed` state, so the crash point is the last point (fewer than N points), with no crash it returns N. It starts from `ship.state` and is bit-identical to real flight with the same input. It takes any N.
- Prediction snapshot: regenerating asteroid chunks on every step cost ~300 ms per 900-step run, so `run` takes an `AsteroidField.near` snapshot around the current position with radius `cover = (|v| + max_speed()) · remaining_steps·DT + q + 2·NARROW_MARGIN`, where `max_speed()` is `AsteroidField.max_speed()` (asteroids move toward the ship too) and `q` is `max(ast_radius_min, ast_radius_max)` (or at least `sync_radius` when `drift_sync` is held). The snapshot is re-taken when `|pos − c0| + |v|·DT + 1 + max_speed()·(t − t0) + q + NARROW_MARGIN` exceeds `cover` (thrust and gravity change `v`, so one start-time bound is not enough), so every asteroid the ship can meet is in it and results equal live flight. The ship only meets asteroids near it, so each step runs on a narrowed copy (entries within `q + NARROW_MARGIN` of a centre, `NARROW_MARGIN` 800) that is rebuilt when `|pos − centre| + |v|·DT + 1 + max_speed()·Δt + q` could exceed `q + NARROW_MARGIN`; each step receives this narrowed copy as the snapshot; the copy is a pure filter, so results are unchanged. A cost note is in `docs/production/tech-debt.md` #6.
- `Prediction` is a `Palette.HALO` `Line2D` after `Ship` in `Run.tscn` (reads the already-stepped state), not a child of `Ship`. It is always shown. The line is computed from `ship.input` with `turn` zeroed (a held rotation would spin the replayed ship and degenerate the line to coasting); the heading target and state are kept. The width is constant screen size (`2 px / cam.zoom.x`, refreshed every frame). Recomputed every `maxi(predict_every, 1)` ticks and at once on the ship's `respawned` signal (a preset load, regeneration or crash respawn, so no stale line), not on `preset_applied`. There is no full-trajectory mode; the Otryv scene will call `ShipPrediction.run` with its own N. The line ends at a crash point (planet surface or hull 0). The line bounces off moons like flight. Recompute cost is `docs/production/tech-debt.md` #6.
- Input actions live in `project.godot` `[input]`: `ship_rotate_left/right` (A / D), `ship_thrust` (W, right trigger), `ship_boost` (Space, joypad button 0), `ship_brake` (S, left trigger; the soft brake), `ship_drift_sync` (Shift, left stick click), `ship_heading_*` (left stick), `camera_zoom_in/out` (wheel, d-pad; `=` / numpad + and `-` / numpad −), `debug_tuning_panel` (F1, handled in the panel's `_unhandled_input`; flight input stays live while it is open). A stick beyond the deadzone sets `has_heading` with a screen-relative target; otherwise A / D give `turn`.
- `Body.tscn` is a view: Node2D with `Body.gd` (`@export var index`), a `Visual` `Polygon2D` (dark side) and its child `Lit` (lit half). Each physics tick it sets `position = GravityField.body_pos(index, GravityField.time())`; it is hidden while `index >= Tuning.field.bodies.size()`. Every body is a flat disc of its world radius at any zoom (`DISC_RADIUS` 12; point counts `PLANET_POINTS` 512 / `PLANET_LIT_POINTS` 256 for the planet and `MOON_POINTS` 64 / `MOON_LIT_POINTS` 32 for moons, built in `_ready`, scaled each `_process` to `planet_surface_radius` for index 0 or `bodies[i].radius`): the planet in `Palette.OBOD_ACCENT`, moons in `Palette.MOON`; `Visual` is `Palette.shade(colour)` and `Lit` is `Palette.lit_half(DISC_RADIUS, lit_n, Palette.LIGHT_DIR)` (`lit_n` the lit count above) in the plain colour (the terminator). The old constant-screen-size planet square, the zero-radius moon marker and `visual_scale` are gone. Params live in `Tuning.field`, not on the node.
- `ShipView.gd` (`class_name`, script of `Ship/Visual`) is the ship look, view-only (reads `ship.input` and `ship.state` each `_process`, never writes flight state). At `_ready` it adds `Flame` and `Glow` polygons. The hull polygon is `Palette.HULL` and both wings `Palette.HULL_SHADE`, set in `_ready`. The flame is off while `soft_brake` or `drift_sync` is held (the `ShipFlight` branch priority: thrust and boost are ignored there). Pure statics: `flame_length(thrust, boost, hold_time, heat, p)` = 0 without thrust or boost, else `FLAME_BASE` 24 × the `ShipFlight` ramp scale (`lerp(ramp_min_fraction, 1, hold_time / ramp_time)`) × the heat falloff above `heat_threshold` × `FLAME_BOOST` 1.6 when boosting; `glow_alpha(heat, p)` = `GLOW_FAINT` 0.15 · heat / `heat_threshold` up to the threshold, then linear to 1 at heat 1 (D36: overheat shown only by the nozzle glow). Flame is a `Palette.DANGER` triangle from the nozzle (`NOZZLE_Y`), its polygon assigned only while the flame length is > 0; the glow is a `Palette.DANGER` disc at the nozzle, visible from heat > 0, with alpha `glow_alpha` and scale growing with heat (`GLOW_SCALE_MIN/MAX`). The cargo is not on the hull: it is the `HUD` hold bar.
- `Carrier.tscn`, `Field.tscn` are empty `Node2D` roots.
